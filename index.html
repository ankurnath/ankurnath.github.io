Research Scientist, Meta · Menlo Park, California · anath@tamu.edu

I'm Ankur Nath, a Research Scientist at Meta, where I develop agentic systems that provide automated support to Facebook users. My PhD research at Texas A&M University, advised by Dr. Alan Kuhnle, focuses on learning-guided combinatorial optimization: combining reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, and large language models to solve large-scale optimization problems efficiently.

Research interests: Operations Research · Combinatorial Optimization · Graph Neural Networks · Reinforcement Learning · Large Language Models
